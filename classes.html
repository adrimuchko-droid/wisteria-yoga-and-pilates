Classes

What you can practise with us and who each format is for.

Regular Yoga & Pilates classes

60-minute instructor-led group classes for beginners and regular practitioners. Groups have a maximum of 10 people.

  • Vinyasa – movement linked smoothly with breath.
  • Hatha – a calmer pace with attention to postures.
  • Ashtanga – a set sequence of postures.
  • Pilates – controlled movement and strength work.

Passes & memberships

Passes for 3, 4 or 8 classes, or unlimited access, valid for a set period. For people who want to practise regularly. Pricing

Specialised classes

  • Mother-and-child yoga
  • Ukrainian-language yoga
  • Outdoor yoga

Private & couples yoga

60-minute sessions for one person or two. For those who want personal instruction or to practise together.

Workshops & Kundalini

Special sessions and themed workshops for those who want a deeper practice. Workshops

Sound healing & breathwork
