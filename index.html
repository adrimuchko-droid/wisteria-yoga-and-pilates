How to start

  1. Choose a class

    Check the schedule or send us a message and we’ll help you decide.

  2. Book a first class

    You book your place online in Fitssey. You can start with a single class.

  3. Continue if it feels right

    If you like the teacher and the group, a pass makes regular practice easier. Later you can try a workshop.

Why Wisteria

Groups of up to 10
Teachers have time to notice each student, offer corrections and adapt the practice.
Yoga and Pilates in one membership
Choose different kinds of practice through the week without joining separate studios.
A consistent teaching team
You get to know your teachers, and they get to know your level, needs and progress.
A clear path from beginner
You know where to start and how to progress without guessing the next step.
Classes in Ukrainian
Regular yoga classes in Ukrainian, a language you can feel at ease in.
Workshops, breathwork, sound
Kundalini, themed workshops, sound healing and breathwork in the same place.

Time for yourself

An hour set aside for you, away from work and messages.

Move with awareness

We link postures with breathing and notice how the body feels.

No pressure

You don’t have to get every pose right or compare yourself with anyone. Work at your own pace.

What Wisteria is not

  • We’re not a high-volume studio. We limit class sizes.
  • We don’t expect flexibility, strength or experience.
  • We don’t promise quick results. We value consistent practice and gradual progress.
